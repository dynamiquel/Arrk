# Reading Room: ebooks and audiobooks

Reading Room is an evaluation-only plugin fixture, not a shipped Arrk feature. It adds a personal ebook and audiobook shelf, distinct from movie watchlists and music playlists.

## Import a book

Open Reading Room and choose Import. Supported fixture formats are EPUB, PDF and M4B. Pick an author and edition before confirming the import. An M4B audiobook is not a music album. The plugin copies imported files to its configured book directory and reports any rejected formats.

## Match an edition

Check author, title, language and ISBN when several editions share a name. Choose the narrated edition for an audiobook rather than a translated text edition. If no edition is correct, keep the import pending and correct the metadata instead of accepting the first match.

## Resume listening

Open the audiobook and choose Resume. Progress is saved per account and recording edition. A different narration has a different timeline, so progress is not copied automatically. This does not alter Continue Watching for movies or the last-played position of a music track.

## Export reading notes

Open a book's Notes and choose Export. The export contains that account's highlights and annotations, not the book file. PDF exports and EPUB highlights may use different page or location references. Export notes before removing an edition whose annotations you want to preserve.

## Protect a book from shelf cleanup

Mark the book Keep in Reading Room. This excludes that plugin's book from its own shelf cleanup, not Arrk's movie or album cleanup. Reading Room uses its own retention settings. A favourite in a music playlist is unrelated to this flag.

## Share a shelf

Invite an existing Arrk account to the selected shelf. Sharing the shelf does not grant administrator access or access to the owner's private notes. Check the shared view while signed in as the invited user. Removing the invitation does not erase that person's own imported books.
