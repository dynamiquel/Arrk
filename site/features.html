Music

A real music library, not a bolt-on.

Arrk owns artists, albums, tracks, playlists and acquisition. Deezer is the identity and metadata provider; torrents and Soulseek bring the audio; Navidrome plays it. Albums are always identified before they are placed, so tags, artwork and folder names actually match.

  • Discovery and library share one item model
  • Album, artist and track requests
  • Torrent and Soulseek acquisition, with fallback
  • Playlists, Deezer subscriptions and editorial picks
  • Synced lyrics fetched in the background
  • Full-screen player with an OS media session

Playback

Press play and stay in Arrk.

Jellyfin negotiates and produces the stream; Arrk gives you a clean player on top. Video, audio-track and subtitle selection all work in the browser — including content that needs a remux or transcode.

  • In-site player with HLS and hardware-friendly profiles
  • Text subtitles switch instantly; image subtitles burn in
  • Continue Watching merges progress and next-up per series
  • Near-end counts as finished, so the next episode is ready
  • Next-episode autoplay without leaving fullscreen
  • Music player with queue, full-screen view and lyrics

Downloads

Download management that knows when to stop.

Transmission and slskd feed one downloads page. Arrk watches speed, peers, errors and storage — then applies seeding goals and cleanup rules. It holds off when the connection itself is degraded, so a bad line never causes a deletion spree.

  • Unified torrent and Soulseek transfer list
  • Slow, stalled and errored torrent cleanup
  • Ratio and time-based seeding goals
  • Storage thresholds pause and clean up safely
  • Speed-test gate before any slow/stalled penalty
  • Optional VPN routing for downloads and Soulseek

Bring the household in.

Arrk is the account authority for the stack. Give everyone their own login, history and watchlists, let them request media, and keep moderation with the admins. Arrk can also act as an OpenID Connect provider for players.

  • Admin and User roles, with pending-account approval
  • OIDC provider for Jellyfin and other clients
  • Discord and MyAnimeList sign-in / linking
  • Requests with quality, scope and moderation
  • Auto-approval by user or role

Lifecycle

Your library, on your terms.

Every play and listen feeds one per-user history. That history powers watchlists, Continue Watching and a calm stale-media workflow: warn first, show what can be reclaimed, and delete only if you explicitly ask it to.

  • Per-user watch and listen history
  • Watchlists with external lists from MyAnimeList
  • Per-season TV staleness, album-level music staleness
  • Bookmarks and pins to extend or remove the clock
  • Admin view of reclaimable space
  • Automatic cleanup is opt-in and never surprises you

The supporting cast

The details that make it feel finished.

Smaller features that remove the usual paper cuts — each one optional, each one able to fall back to a sensible default.

Subtitles, covered

One dispatcher across built-in OpenSubtitles and optional Bazarr. Status is provider-independent, new imports get their configured languages automatically, and the player can request a language without leaving playback.

Notifications, routed

Discord and generic webhooks, with per-category routing and delivery-only excludes. Everything still lands in the in-app inbox and the full event feed.

Local imports

Already own the file? Upload it from discovery or the library. Arrk stages it, asks the library manager what it can parse, shows a match score and imports only after you confirm.

MyAnimeList

Link or sign in with MAL, browse and edit your list, and sync completed episodes and scores. Season mapping is automatic, and Plan to Watch appears as a watchlist.

Dashboard tiles

Every module can contribute tiles. Arrange them per account, switch between layouts, and choose a text size that is comfortable without zooming the browser.

AI

Ask about your library, troubleshoot a download or learn how to use Arrk. The assistant uses your Medium model, while lighter background tasks can use Low. Use a cloud or local model, and decide which tasks AI helps with. Arrk works without it, and deleting media still needs your confirmation.
